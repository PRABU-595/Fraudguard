The Modern Fraud Landscape

Problem Statement: Limitations of Legacy Systems

High-Velocity Botnet Attacks

Attackers utilize headless browsers and credential-stuffing botnets executing hundreds of micro-transactions per second, bypassing asynchronous batch review systems.

GPS Spoofing & Impossible Travel

Financial systems lack real-time correlation between reported device GPS fixes and cellular base station coordinates, failing to detect transatlantic travel anomalies.

Analyst Alert Fatigue & MTTR Breach

SOC analysts navigate fragmented tables with zero automated entity linkage, leading to high Mean Time To Resolution (MTTR) and breached SLAs.

Analytics Freezing & Latency Lag

Quadratic time-series string date parsing locks CPU threads during 7D and 30D volume queries, crippling operator responsiveness during active threat bursts.

The Engineering Blueprint

Proposed Solution: FraudGuard Autonomous SOC

Sub-15ms Heuristic & ML Evaluation

Concurrent risk calculation categorizing events into ALLOW (<40), REVIEW (40-79), and BLOCK (≥80), streamed in real-time over Server-Sent Events.

Cell-Tower Haversine Triangulation

Calculates great-circle distance between transaction coordinates and mobile cell towers (MCC/MNC/LAC/CID) to detect impossible travel speeds (>900 km/h).

SLA-Governed Case Management

Strict 60-minute countdown SLA with 4-Eyes dual-control confirmation for releasing high-risk incidents and stable deterministic CASE-XXXX identifiers.

Amazon Bedrock GenAI Copilot

Autonomous multi-layered telemetry synthesis generating plain-English incident briefings, audit explanations, and FINCEN-ready SAR drafts.
